2. If you just browse

To read the calendar, the blog or use the game we do not ask for any personal data and we use no profiling cookies, ads or third-party analytics.

  • Technical browsing data. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which logs technical data such as IP address, browser and pages requested, for security. Legal basis: legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f)). GitHub keeps these logs for as long as its security rules require; we use them for nothing else. You can object (Art. 21) by writing to us.
  • Browser storage. Light/dark theme, your tournament plan, diary and game progress are stored only in your browser (localStorage); we cannot see them. They are technical tools needed for the features you use, so no consent is required. You can delete them by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • Fonts, images and code are served by our own site: no request goes to Google Fonts or other external services while you browse.

3. If you create an account (members' area)

  • Data: email; account creation and last login dates; technical login data (IP address, browser) logged by the authentication service; your statement that you are 18+ and have read this notice, with date and version; the tournament plan and diary you choose to save.
  • Why: to let you log in with an email link (no password) and keep your plan and diary across devices. Legal basis: performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • How long: until you delete the account, which you can do at any time from the members' area: email, plan, diary and consents are deleted immediately. Technical login logs remain with the provider (Supabase) only as long as the service keeps them for security.
  • Mandatory or not: an account is optional and the site works without it. If you create one, your email is necessary: without it we cannot send you the login link.

4. Newsletter (only if you choose it)

In the members' area you can tick a box to receive weekend tournament updates and festival alerts. We use your email only for this. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), recorded with date and text version. You can withdraw it at any time by unticking the box or via the link in every email; withdrawal does not affect what was done before (Art. 7(3)). The newsletter contains no gambling advertising.

5. If you write to us

If you write to info@yugrind.com we use your email and message only to reply and handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)), keeping them no longer than 24 months unless needed to defend a right.

We make no automated decisions about you and build no advertising profiles. The members' area is for people aged 18+ only; accounts of minors are deleted.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of the EU country where you live.

WSOP Circuit Sanremo 2026: tournament schedule

The WSOP Circuit takes place at Casinò di Sanremo from 20 to 30 November 2026. The festival features eleven days of tournaments and 11 WSOP rings.

November 9, 2026 · YUGRIND

The WSOP Circuit Sanremo 2026 will take place at the Casinò di Sanremo from 20 to 30 November, in collaboration with TexApoker. The festival features eleven days of tournaments, with 11 WSOP rings to be awarded.

Tournament programme

  • Mini Main Event: €300 buy-in, with four flights: Friday 20 November at 13:00 and 20:00, and Saturday 21 November at the same times.
  • Mystery Bounty: €550 buy-in, starting on 22 November.
  • Colossus: €300 buy-in, with starting days from 22 to 25 November.
  • High Roller: €2,500 buy-in, scheduled for 25 November.
  • Main Event satellite: €180 buy-in, scheduled at 21:00 each evening from 20 to 27 November, except Tuesday.
  • Main Event: €1,500 buy-in, scheduled from 26 to 30 November. It has four flights: three afternoon flights on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, plus a final chance on Saturday evening.
  • Deepstack: €1,000 buy-in, Sunday 29 November at 15:00.
  • Omaha: €400 buy-in, Sunday 29 November at 19:30.
  • Bigstack: €300 buy-in, Monday 30 November at 15:00.

All events allow unlimited re-entries. A 6% staff service charge is included in the prize pools.

WSOP rings and prize package

Each ring winner will receive a package worth $5,000 for WSOP Paradise Bahamas 2026.

Casinò di Sanremo

The Casinò di Sanremo is a partner of the World Series of Poker circuit and TexApoker. It has previously hosted the Italian leg of the European Poker Tour and the Italian Poker Tour, and is a partner of the Italian Poker Open.

Source: Assopoker

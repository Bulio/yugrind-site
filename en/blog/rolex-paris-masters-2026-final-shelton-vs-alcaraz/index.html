Rolex Paris Masters 2026 Final: Shelton vs Alcaraz

Ben Shelton and Carlos Alcaraz meet in the Rolex Paris Masters final at La Défense Arena, Paris, on 8 November 2026.

November 9, 2026 · YUGRIND

The ATP Masters 1000 final in Paris, the Rolex Paris Masters, takes place on 8 November 2026 at 15:00 in La Défense Arena.

The finalists

American Ben Shelton faces Spain’s Carlos Alcaraz. Shelton reached the final after three-set matches against Rinderknech, Paul and Luca Van Assche. He also beat Casper Ruud 2-0. Alcaraz beat Ruud 2-0.

Their head-to-head record is 3-1 in Alcaraz’s favour, with five meetings in total. They met once on indoor synthetic courts at the 2024 Laver Cup.

Service statistics

In their last two meetings, Alcaraz won 34.1% of the points on Shelton’s serve at Roland Garros 2025 and 34.8% at the US Open 2026. Alcaraz’s career average for points won on his own serve is 42%.

Television coverage

In Italy, the TV rights belong to Sky. The match will be shown on Sky Sport Uno and Sky Sport Tennis, with streaming available on Sky Go and Now TV.

The ATP Finals will be held in Turin, and the Davis Cup Final Eight will be held in Bologna.

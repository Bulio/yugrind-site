Elena Yasyuchenya wins $3k One-W Championship in Kyrenia

Elena Yasyuchenya won the $3k One-W Championship in Kyrenia for $35,500. The tournament had 42 entries and a $112,518 prize pool.

November 10, 2026 · YUGRIND

The $3k One-W Championship (Triton One Northern Cyprus Series) took place from November 5–15 at Merit Poker, Merit Royal Diamond Hotel & Casino, Kyrenia. Elena Yasyuchenya (Russia) won the event and the $35,500 first-place prize.

Tournament details

  • Buy-in range: $2,000–$15,000, with satellites starting at $1,500.
  • Total entries: 42, comprising 27 original entries and 15 re-entries.
  • Prize pool: $112,518.
  • Paid places: 7.

Final table highlights

When five players remained, the average stack was 17 big blinds. Tang Lili was the chip leader at the money bubble. Jessica Teusl had 11 big blinds at one point and finished fourth for $12,400.

Diana Kalietina went all-in with jacks against Tang Lili’s queens. Kseniia Kolotiuk doubled up against Jessica Teusl’s tens. Ye Yi finished fifth, eliminated with one big blind left.

Heads-up, Elena Yasyuchenya prevailed with a 4 against Kseniia Kolotiuk’s aces to win the title.

Adam Wilkinson wins GUKPT Blackpool Main Event

Adam Wilkinson won the GUKPT Blackpool Main Event, which recorded 190 entries and a £237,500 prize pool. The GUKPT Grand Final 2026 is scheduled for London.

November 10, 2026 · YUGRIND

Adam Wilkinson won the GUKPT Blackpool Main Event and received £55,035. The tournament in Blackpool recorded 190 entries, a £1,250 buy-in and a total prize pool of £237,500.

Final table results

Michael Whitehead finished second and received £42,435. Chris Guertin placed third for £26,580, while Christopher Wood finished fourth for £17,790. Jon Kalmar came fifth, receiving £12,210.

The remaining finishers, from sixth to ninth, were:

  • Sixth: Chris Cox, £8,880.
  • Seventh: Benjamin Hill, £6,980.
  • Eighth: Trevor Reardon, £5,920.
  • Ninth: Peter Charalambous, £5,200.

Grand Final 2026

The GUKPT Grand Final 2026 is scheduled for 19 to 29 November at the Grosvenor Victoria in London. Its Main Event has a £2,000 buy-in and a £500,000 guarantee.

The GUKPT Grand Final London will take place in London. In 2025, Alan Stearn won the Grand Final Main Event and received £125,645.

Source: Poker News Daily

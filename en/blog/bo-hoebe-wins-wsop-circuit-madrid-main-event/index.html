Bo Hoebe Wins WSOP Circuit Madrid Main Event

Bo Hoebe won the 2026 WSOP Circuit Madrid €1,500 Main Event, defeating Jose “Nacho” Barbero heads-up to claim his second WSOPC gold ring.

November 8, 2026 · YUGRIND

The €1,500 No-Limit Hold’em Main Event at the 2026 World Series of Poker Circuit Madrid drew 918 entries and generated a prize pool worth more than $1.3 million. The final table was set on 31 October at the Casino Gran Via Madrid.

Main Event results

The top 140 finishers received prize money. Bo Hoebe of the Netherlands won €207,000 (US$231,840), his second WSOPC gold ring and 912 Card Player Player-of-the-Year points. Jose “Nacho” Barbero of Argentina finished runner-up for $145,600 and earned 760 POY points.

Barbero doubled up early with pocket aces against Luca Martin’s pocket kings. He then eliminated Fausto Munz with pocket aces against A-Q, Alonso with pocket aces against A♣K♣, and Per Hildebrand with pocket aces against J♠J♣. Barbero was dealt pocket aces five times in roughly 1.5 hours of final-table play.

Hoebe’s first knockout of the day was Martin, who finished ninth for $15,904. Hoebe made top two pair. He later doubled with pocket queens to eliminate Tom-Aksel Bedell, who finished third for $100,800.

Hoebe and Barbero began heads-up play with Barbero holding approximately five chips for every four in Hoebe’s stack. In the final hand, Hoebe held Q♠10♠ against Barbero’s K♠2♣. The board was A♥Q♦2♦, and the Q♥ river gave Hoebe the winning hand.

Final-table standings

  • 1st: Bo Hoebe (Netherlands), €207,000 (US$231,840)
  • 2nd: Jose “Nacho” Barbero (Argentina), $145,600
  • 3rd: Tom-Aksel Bedell (Norway), $100,800
  • 4th: Cristóbal Aguilera, $70,560
  • 5th: Per Hildebrand (Sweden), $50,400
  • 6th: Aliaksandr Shylko (Belarus), $36,960
  • 7th: Alonso, $26,880
  • 8th: Fausto Munz, $20,384
  • 9th: Luca Martin, $15,904

Hoebe previously won a WSOPC gold ring at the 2023 WSOP International Circuit Netherlands stop. Barbero has more than $30 million in career cashes, including six seven-figure cashes and 62 six-figure cashes.

Hustler Casino Live: Dumbledore wins $389,200

Dumbledore was the biggest winner on Hustler Casino Live on October 30, 2026, finishing up $389,200. Ethan ‘Rampage’ Yau lost $176,200.

November 8, 2026 · YUGRIND

On October 30, 2026, Dumbledore finished the Hustler Casino Live cash-game broadcast with a net gain of $389,200. Ethan ‘Rampage’ Yau, a two-time World Series of Poker bracelet winner, finished as the second-biggest loser, down $176,200. More than $2 million was on the table when the stream concluded.

The hand between Yau and Dumbledore

The hand was played with $100-$200 blinds and two straddles in effect. Peter Wang limped from the big blind with 8♠ 7♥. Dumbledore raised to $4,000 from the first straddle holding A♥ 10♠. Yau, in the second straddle with K♥ K♦, re-raised to $15,000.

The flop was 7♣ 6♦ 2♣. Dumbledore checked and Yau checked behind. The turn was 10♦, giving Dumbledore top pair. He bet $20,000 and Yau called, bringing the pot to $71,100.

The river was 10♥, giving Dumbledore three of a kind. He over-bet $125,000 and Yau called. Dumbledore won the $321,100 pot, raising his stack to over $627,000.
